# QED–DST Bridge

Both systems remain sovereign and unmodified. The bridge is explicitly asymmetric.

**Direction A — inflate.** QED statement becomes DST intake. Ornament may be added;
context, framing, and candidate representation classes are supplied.

**Direction B — compress.** DST traversal becomes QED submission. Ornament must be
stripped. Open residue is carried across explicitly and held, never absorbed.

The asymmetry is the content. Same discipline governs [[Bridge Map]].
